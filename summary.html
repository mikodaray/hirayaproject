Chapter 1: Banaag

50 pages. Status: DRAFT storyboard.

The short version. Jeff looks cool, but his classmates think he is weird because he loves Filipino folklore. After a fight with the school's popular girl, he and his friends get detention in an old storage room. They find a glowing bamboo tube with Baybayin writing, Jeff reads it out loud, and the four fall through time to Butuan in the year 1003. The Rajah sees Jeff's necklace, and it turns out to be one of ten magic Agimats. At the end he demands it, and Jeff says no.

What happens, page by page

  1. Pages 1 to 4

    Meet the four

    Durian City at dawn. Jeff walks to school with a folklore book, and classmates whisper about him. Hal runs in with pandesal, Mel plays the violin on the steps, and Nikki arrives with her group and calls them the nerd, the giant, and the pocket edition.

  2. Pages 5 to 8

    Jeff steps in

    Nikki takes Hal's glasses. Jeff stands between them and she swings at him. On the page 7 splash, Jeff catches her wrist without looking. The bell rings, and Nikki glances back for half a second.

  3. Pages 9 to 10

    Sir Archie's lesson

    In homeroom, Sir Archie teaches about the Sangkapuluan, the old name of the islands, and the ten Agimats said to sleep in them. Jeff's hand goes to his necklace, a gift from his father.

  4. Pages 11 to 14

    Detention

    All four are sent to clean an old storage room. Sir Archie warns them not to touch the cabinets at the back. They joke, clean, and for a moment even Nikki laughs with them.

  5. Pages 15 to 20

    The tube

    The necklace warms up and leads them to the locked cabinet. The lock crumbles and they find a glowing bamboo tube with Baybayin writing. Jeff reads "Hiraya manawari" aloud, Mel plays along, and golden script rises from the tube.

  6. Pages 21 to 26

    The fall through time

    The room shakes and the words pull them in. Hal and Nikki grab each other. They fall through a tunnel of light while years flash by and stop at 1003, then burst out of the sky above a river.

  7. Pages 27 to 31

    Butuan

    They splash into the river and swim to the bank. A child sees them and runs. They climb a hill, and page 31 is the big splash: the busy river port of Butuan at dawn.

  8. Pages 32 to 37

    First contact

    Warriors surround them. Jeff says "friends" in modern Bisaya, which sounds like "fish bait" in the old tongue. Mel plays his violin and the spears lower. Hal becomes a celebrity because of his glasses, and they are taken through the market to the Rajah.

  9. Pages 38 to 41

    The Rajah's hall

    Jeff says they are "visitors", which sounds like "ghosts", and the court panics. The elder explains the mistake. Then the elder sees Jeff's necklace and says it is one of the ten. The Rajah makes the four his guests, maybe.

  10. Pages 42 to 46

    A quiet night

    They wear borrowed clothes and eat together. The tube is gone and nobody knows the way home. Jeff remembers his father's words, and the elder warns that not all ten Agimats are friendly. In the morning Nikki quietly thanks Jeff.

  11. Pages 47 to 50

    The demand

    The Rajah summons Jeff and demands the necklace. His friends stand beside him. Jeff steps forward in the same stance as page 7 and says, "No, Rajah." The necklace hums, golden Baybayin rises around the four, and the chapter ends on "To be continued."
