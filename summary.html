Chapter 1: Banaag

50 pages planned. Pages 1 to 10 approved, pages 11 to 28 a new draft (2026-11-12), pages 29 to 50 still to be added. Work in progress.

The short version. Jeff looks cool, but his classmates think he is weird because he loves Filipino folklore. After a day of school, Dan Macabebe, a big bully, picks on Jared Callao, a quiet punk student whose father is a drunk who beats his mother. Jared walks home bruised, thinking about what waits for him, and throws a stone at a tree. It wakes an Agta, a spirit that feeds on hatred. While Jeff enjoys a loud, funny evening with his family, the Agta watches Jared, and when Jared turns off his light it merges with his shadow. Jared falls asleep and the dream begins. The rest is still to be written.

What happens, page by page

  1. Pages 1 to 4

    Meet the four

    Durian City at dawn. Jeff walks to school with a folklore book, and classmates whisper about him. Hal runs in with pandesal, Mel plays the violin on the steps, and Nikki arrives with her friends.

  2. Pages 5 to 8

    Jeff steps in

    Nikki takes Hal's glasses. Jeff stands between them. On the page 7 splash, Jeff catches her wrist without looking. The bell rings.

  3. Pages 9 to 10

    Sir Archie's lesson

    In homeroom, Sir Archie teaches about the Sangkapuluan and the ten Agimats said to sleep in the islands. Jeff's hand goes to his necklace.

  4. Pages 11 to 13

    Jared alone

    After school the friends split up at the gate. Dan Macabebe finds Jared, a quiet punk student, alone and beats him up. Students walk past and no one helps.

  5. Pages 14 to 15

    The stone

    Jared walks home with slight bruises and thinks about his drunk father and his terrified mother waiting at home. He throws a stone at an old tree. It wakes an Agta, a mean spirit that feeds on hatred and makes it grow.

  6. Pages 16 to 19

    Jeff's home

    Jeff says "I'm home" and his twin sisters Gail and Janelle hug him while he complains in a cute way. At dinner Mama teases him about school and girlfriends.

  7. Pages 20 to 22

    Papa and the jeepney

    Papa, a jeepney driver, asks Jeff to help fix the jeepney. They talk about his high marks, and Jeff says he studies hard so he is free to learn Arnis from his father.

  8. Pages 23 to 24

    Jared at home

    In his room, Jared hears his father shouting downstairs and can't draw. He is taller than his father now, but he can't stop him, and he can't even stand up to Dan. He is full of hate.

  9. Pages 25 to 27

    The Agta moves in

    The Agta watches from a tree outside Jared's house and says, "Let me help you with that." When Jared turns off the light, it jumps through the window and merges with his shadow.

  10. Page 28

    The dream begins

    Jared goes to bed and closes his eyes. A tiny light grows in the dark, and the dream sequence starts.

  11. Pages 29 to 50

    To be added

    The dream sequence and the rest of the arc. The creator will add these a few details at a time.
