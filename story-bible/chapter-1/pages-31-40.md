# Chapter 1 Storyboard: Pages 31 to 40

Status: DRAFT. Part 4 of 5. The grand reveal of Butuan, first contact, Mel's music, and the court.
Panel sizes: WIDE = full width strip, LARGE = about half the page, TALL = vertical strip, SMALL = about one sixth, INSET = tiny panel on top of another.
Research flavor used here: balangay boats, gold work, trade ships from other lands, crucibles. See story-bible/05-research-notes.md. All of it is flavor only, history comes second.

---

## Page 31 (SPLASH, full page): Butuan
Layout: one full page image.

**1. FULL PAGE.** The grand reveal. A huge river port at dawn. Dozens of plank boats with striped sails, joined with wooden pegs, crowd the water. Stilt houses line the banks. People wear gold ornaments that flash in the early sun. Foreign trade ships with different sails sit further out. A carved wooden hall with a gold decorated roof sits on the hill in the distance. In the foreground, small, four silhouettes stand at the top of the bank: a tall one with spiky hair, a short one with glasses, a big one holding a violin, a girl with her wet hair blowing.
CAPTION: "Butuan. The year 1003."

Art note: this is the biggest image in the chapter. It is the answer to the whole premise. No dialogue.

## Page 34 (6 panels): Tense, and the first misunderstanding
Layout: small panel, medium panel, two small panels, a small panel, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. SMALL.** Hal, behind Jeff.
HAL: "Jeff. Spears."

**2. MEDIUM.** Jeff raises his hands slowly, cool as ever, and speaks slowly in Bisaya (shown here in English).
JEFF: "Good morning! We are friends!"
CAPTION: "(Jeff used the modern Bisaya word for 'friends'. In the old tongue it sounds like their word for 'fish bait'.)"

**3. SMALL.** The lead warrior, frowning.
WARRIOR: [Bait? He says they are bait?]

**4. SMALL.** A second warrior eyes them up and down.
WARRIOR 2: [Bait for what? For us?]
HAL: "Why are they looking at us like lunch?"

**5. SMALL.** Nikki hides behind Mel, voice tiny.
NIKKI: "Mel. Do something."
MEL: "What can I do?"
NIKKI: "ANYTHING!"

**6. LARGE.** Mel looks at the violin. Looks at the spears. A small, brave smile.

## Page 35 (6 panels): Mel's music
Layout: large panel on top, three small panels, a medium panel, a wide panel at the bottom.

**1. LARGE.** Mel steps forward and raises the violin. A soft, gentle melody. Glowing notes ripple outward through the crowd like water.

**2. SMALL.** The warriors flinch, then freeze and listen.

**3. SMALL.** The child lowers his net, enchanted.

**4. SMALL.** A woman with a basket on her head stops walking.

**5. MEDIUM.** Jeff glances at Mel, impressed.
JEFF: "Mel. That's it."

**6. WIDE.** The circle of spears slowly lowers. A bubble of calm spreads outward. Notes drift toward the harbor.

## Page 37 (6 panels): Through the market

## Page 38 (5 panels): The Rajah's hall
Layout: wide panel, two small panels, a medium panel, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** Up the hill. A wooden hall with carved posts and a gold decorated roof.

**2. SMALL.** Guards. Carved wood. Incense smoke at the door.

**3. SMALL.** Hal, whispering.
HAL: "Do we bow?"
MEL: "I'll bow twice."

**4. MEDIUM.** Nikki.
NIKKI: "I'm not bowing to..."
JEFF: "Nikki. Bow."
She bows, fast.

**5. LARGE.** The Rajah on the dais. Gold armbands, a calm and severe face. Beside him stands an elder advisor with a staff. (The Rajah is based on Kiling, the ruler named in Chinese records of this period. Design and personality are DRAFT.)

## Page 39 (6 panels): Audience, and the second misunderstanding
Layout: wide panel, two small panels, a medium panel, a small panel, a small panel.

**1. WIDE.** The four kneel in a line, wet, muddy, in school uniforms. A strong contrast with the gold hall.

**2. SMALL.** The Rajah.
RAJAH: [Who are you, who arrive on my river with no boat?]

**3. SMALL.** Jeff, catching the meaning.
JEFF: "He's asking who we are."

**4. MEDIUM.** Jeff stands halfway and speaks in slow, careful Bisaya (shown in English).
JEFF: "I am Jeff. We are only visitors."
CAPTION: "(Jeff's word for 'visitors' is now their word for 'ghosts'. Words change a lot in a thousand years.)"

**5. SMALL.** The court erupts. A man drops his basket.
COURT: [GHOSTS!]
HAL: "What did you say?!"
JEFF: "I said visitors!"

**6. SMALL.** The Rajah is unbothered, eyes fixed on Jeff's chest. The elder leans in and whispers to him.
ELDER: [The boy means guests. The word has moved with the years.]
