# Chapter 1 Storyboard: Pages 21 to 30

Status: DRAFT. Part 3 of 5. The portal sequence, the fall through time, the river, and the first sight of Butuan.
Panel sizes: WIDE = full width strip, LARGE = about half the page, TALL = vertical strip, SMALL = about one sixth, INSET = tiny panel on top of another.

---

## Page 21 (5 panels): It pulls them
Layout: wide panel, two small panels, a medium panel, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** The room shakes. Boxes rattle, dust whirls, the old fan spins on its own.

**2. SMALL.** Hal.
HAL: "JEFF, STOP!"
JEFF: "I can't! The words are pulling me!"

**3. SMALL.** Mel, wide eyed, still playing. He can't stop either.

**4. MEDIUM.** Nikki grabs Hal's arm without thinking. Hal grabs back.

**5. LARGE.** Ribbons of golden script wrap around all four of them.

---

## Page 22 (5 panels): Four faces
Layout: four tall vertical strips side by side across the top two thirds, one wide panel at the bottom.

**1. TALL.** Jeff, determined, eyes forward.

**2. TALL.** Hal, terrified, clutching his glasses.

**3. TALL.** Mel, amazed, violin pressed to his chest.

**4. TALL.** Nikki, scared, eyes squeezed shut.

**5. WIDE.** Outside the window the sky bends like glass. The school windows crack with golden light.

---

## Page 23 (4 panels): The last word
Layout: small panel, a thin black strip, a small panel, a large panel.

**1. SMALL.** Jeff, whispering.
JEFF: "...manawari."

**2. THIN STRIP, full width.** Pure black. Silence.

**3. SMALL.** A single sound.
SFX: ...

**4. LARGE.** White flash fills the panel. The ribbons form a spiral.
SFX: KRAAATHOOOOM

---

## Page 24 (5 panels): The fall
Layout: wide panel, three small panels, a medium panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** A tunnel of light. Chalkboards, desks, and school fragments float past. The four are falling.

**2. SMALL.** Hal, screaming. His glasses float off his nose but stay on by a miracle.
HAL: "AAAAAAAAAAAH!"

**3. SMALL.** Mel curls around his violin to protect it.

**4. SMALL.** Nikki screaming. Then she sees Hal's hand holding hers. She stares at it.

**5. MEDIUM.** Jeff reaches for all of them.
JEFF: "Hold on to each other!"

---

## Page 25 (5 panels): Years
Layout: large panel on top, three small panels, a wide panel at the bottom.

**1. LARGE.** Glowing numbers rush past like falling stars: 2026, 1900, 1521, 1200. They settle on 1003.

**2. SMALL.** Jeff, to himself.
JEFF: "The year... it's going backward."

**3. SMALL.** Hal, green faced.
HAL: "I'M GOING TO THROW UP."

**4. SMALL.** Mel.
MEL: "Please don't throw up on the violin."

**5. WIDE.** At the end of the tunnel: sunlight, blue sky, and far below, a river.

---

## Page 26 (4 panels): Out of the sky
Layout: wide panel, two small panels, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** The four burst out of the sky above a river delta, trailing golden ribbons.

**2. SMALL.** Below: wooden boats, small people looking up.

**3. SMALL.** Nikki.
NIKKI: "THERE'S WATER!"
JEFF: "That's good!"
HAL: "I CAN'T SWIM VERY WELL!"
JEFF: "Then hold your breath!"

**4. LARGE.** Jeff dives to catch Hal and grabs his hand.

---

## Page 27 (5 panels): Splash
Layout: one tall panel on the right, four stacked small panels on the left.

**1. TALL.** The impact. A huge splash on the river. Birds scatter.
SFX: SPLOOOSH

**2. SMALL.** Underwater: bubbles. Mel's blonde hair floats like seaweed. He holds the violin to his chest.

**3. SMALL.** Hal sinking, eyes wide.

**4. SMALL.** Jeff swims, catches Hal's drifting glasses.

**5. SMALL.** Nikki reaches for Hal's other hand and pulls him up with Jeff.

---

## Page 28 (5 panels): Surface
Layout: wide panel, three small panels, a medium panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** Four heads pop up on a calm river near a mangrove bank. Early morning light.

**2. SMALL.** Hal, coughing.
HAL: "I'm alive! I'm alive!"

**3. SMALL.** Nikki, soaked and furious at her hair.
NIKKI: "My hair!"
Then, quietly: "Hal. Are you okay?"
Hal blinks, surprised.

**4. SMALL.** Mel holds the violin above the water like a trophy.
MEL: "She's okay. We're all okay."

**5. MEDIUM.** Jeff, dripping. The necklace clings to his chest and the glow fades.
JEFF: "Guys... look."

---

## Page 29 (6 panels): First contact
Layout: wide panel, two small panels, a medium panel, a small panel, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** They crawl onto a muddy bank beside a wooden pier. A child with a fishing net stares at them.

**2. SMALL.** The child's point of view: four strangers in strange wet clothes, one with purple hair.

**3. SMALL.** The child shouts and runs.
CHILD: [Strangers! They fell from the sky!]
(Spoken in an old tongue, shown in brackets.)

**4. MEDIUM.** Hal.
HAL: "Did he just say sky?"
JEFF: "I understood him. It's Bisaya, but with words nobody uses anymore."
HAL: "I only understood half of that."

**5. SMALL.** Nikki.
NIKKI: "Okay. Where are we?"

**6. LARGE.** They climb up the bank toward the pier, mud on their knees, Mel pulling Hal.

---

## Page 30 (6 panels): The top of the hill
Layout: three small panels, a thin wide panel, a small panel, a medium panel.

**1. SMALL.** Hal slips. Mel pulls him up by the backpack.

**2. SMALL.** Off panel sounds drift in: gongs, voices, rushing water, a hundred things at once.

**3. SMALL.** Jeff reaches the top first. The wind lifts his hair.

**4. THIN WIDE.** Close up of Jeff's eyes. Gold light in them.

**5. SMALL.** Jeff's thought:
CAPTION: "This isn't our school. This isn't our city. This isn't even our century."

**6. MEDIUM.** Hal from below.
HAL: "Jeff? What do you see?"
