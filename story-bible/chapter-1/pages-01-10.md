# Chapter 1 Storyboard: Pages 1 to 10

Status: DRAFT. Part 1 of 5. Introduces the four main characters, the bully conflict, the splash page for Jeff, and the start of homeroom.
Panel sizes: WIDE = full width strip, LARGE = about half the page, TALL = vertical strip, SMALL = about one sixth, INSET = tiny panel on top of another.

---

## Page 1 (4 panels): Opening, meet Jeff
Layout: wide strip on top, a tall panel and a small panel side by side in the middle, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE, top third.** Dawn over Durian City, a state of the art city like Makati. Glass towers catching the golden sunrise, wide avenues, colorful jeepneys driving along them, a few palm and durian trees along the street.
CAPTION: "Durian City, Mindanao. 6:42 AM."

**2. TALL, middle left.** The gate of Durian Public High School. Flag, painted sign, students trickling in, the modern skyline behind the school.

**3. SMALL, middle right.** Close up: a necklace swinging against a blue uniform shirt. One tiny glint. No explanation.

**4. LARGE LOW ANGLE, bottom half.** Jeff walks toward the camera. Ube colored hair in the wind, one hand in his pocket, the other holding a thick book on Philippine folklore. Tall, lean, effortless.
CAPTION: "Jefferson Victorio. Nineteen. Everyone says he's the cool one."
CAPTION: "Nobody mentions he reads about ancient spirits for breakfast."

---

## Page 2 (5 panels): Meet Hal
Layout: wide strip on top, two small panels, then a tall panel on the right and a large panel on the left.

**1. WIDE, top.** Behind Jeff, a tiny figure sprints through the crowd, a backpack bigger than he is.
SFX: TAP TAP TAP TAP

**2. SMALL.** Jeff does not turn around. Small smirk.
JEFF: "Three. Two. One."

**3. SMALL.** Hal crashes into Jeff's back. Glasses slide down his nose.
SFX: THUD
HAL: "JEFF! Wait up!"

**4. TALL, right.** Hal's face close up. Brown hair, round glasses, big eyes. He is very cute and very offended.
HAL: "You walked past the bakery again! I texted you six times!"

**5. LARGE, left.** The height gap. Jeff looks down, Hal looks up, a paper bag of pandesal held high like a trophy.
HAL: "I got your favorite."
JEFF: "You're a legend."
CAPTION: "Hal Bautista. Five feet flat. Jeff's best friend. Loudest voice in any room."

---

## Page 3 (6 panels): Meet Mel
Layout: wide strip on top, a tall panel on the left, three stacked small panels in the middle, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE, top.** Violin music drifts across the school grounds. Students stop on the stairs. Glowing musical notes weave through the air.

**2. TALL, left.** Mel on the school steps, eyes closed, long blonde hair over his shoulder, violin tucked under his chin. A very big, very gentle guy.

**3. SMALL.** Jeff and Hal arrive at the bottom of the steps. Hal waves with the pandesal bag.

**4. SMALL.** Mel opens his eyes and smiles without stopping the music.

**5. SMALL.** Mel's bow slows, then stops.
MEL: "Good morning. I made something new."

**6. LARGE, bottom.** The trio together. Hal has climbed two steps to reach Mel's shoulder height. Jeff leans on the railing with his book.
JEFF: "It sounds like sunrise."
HAL: "It sounds like pandesal."
MEL: "Both are correct."
CAPTION: "Mel Charlie Abaldado. Six foot two. The violin is the one thing that always makes sense to him."

---

## Page 4 (5 panels): Meet Nikki
Layout: wide strip on top, a small panel and a tall panel, a large low angle panel in the middle, a medium panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE, top.** The gate opens. Wind. A group of girls in white blouses and skirts. No dialogue.

**2. SMALL.** Hal freezes mid bite.
HAL: "...No."

**3. TALL.** Students step aside, one after another, like a slow wave.

**4. LARGE LOW ANGLE.** Nikki walks forward, hair in the wind, her friends flanking her. She owns the sidewalk.
CAPTION: "Nikki Sharon Legazpi. The most popular girl in school."

**5. MEDIUM, bottom.** She stops in front of the trio. Smug.
NIKKI: "Look. The nerd, the giant, and the pocket edition."

---

## Page 5 (6 panels): Nikki picks on Hal
Layout: small panel, wide panel, three small panels in a row, and a medium panel at the bottom.

**1. SMALL.** Hal bristles, finger raised.
HAL: "I am NOT a pocket edition!"

**2. WIDE.** Nikki plucks his glasses off his face and holds them high. Her friends laugh.

**3. SMALL.** Hal's blurry point of view: smears of color and shapes.

**4. SMALL.** Hal jumping, fingertips short of the glasses.
NIKKI: "Aww. He can't even jump."

**5. SMALL.** Mel lowers the violin, troubled.
MEL: "Nikki, please. Those are his."

**6. MEDIUM, bottom.** Jeff calm. His necklace swings once against his chest.

---

## Page 6 (5 panels): Jeff steps in
Layout: wide panel, two medium panels, a small panel, and a large panel at the bottom that ends on a cliffhanger.

**1. WIDE.** Jeff steps between Nikki and Hal. We see his back. Hal peeks out behind his arm.

**2. MEDIUM.** Jeff, steady.
JEFF: "Give them back."
NIKKI: "Make me."

**3. MEDIUM.** Jeff's smirk.
JEFF: "Do you ever get tired of being this predictable?"
NIKKI: "Do you ever get tired of reading about ghosts?"

**4. SMALL.** Hal, from behind Jeff.
HAL: "They're not ghosts. It's folklore!"
MEL (off panel): "It's technically mythology."

**5. LARGE.** Nikki, furious. Her hand snaps toward Jeff's face, with speed lines.
SFX: WHIP

---

## Page 7 (SPLASH, full page): Jeff proves the point
Layout: one full page image.

**1. FULL PAGE.** Jeff catches Nikki's wrist without even looking at it, one hand, calm. Dramatic angle from slightly below. Behind him Hal's eyes are huge, Mel's bow is frozen mid air, the students are frozen. Leaves and dust float in a shaft of morning sun. Nikki's face is shocked.
JEFF: "Not him."
CAPTION (tiny, bottom corner): "Jeff Victorio never starts a fight. He just never lets his friends stand alone."

Art note: this is the page that sells Jeff as the cool one. Everyone else in the frame is in comic shock, Jeff is the only calm face.

---

## Page 8 (6 panels): Aftermath
Layout: wide panel, two small panels, a medium panel, a small panel, and a wide panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** Silence. The school bell rings.
SFX: KRIIIIING

**2. SMALL.** Nikki yanks her hand free and tosses the glasses to Jeff.
NIKKI: "Whatever. Come on, girls."

**3. SMALL.** Her friends scurry after her.

**4. MEDIUM.** Jeff gently puts the glasses back on Hal and straightens them.
HAL: "Thanks, Jeff."
JEFF: "Don't mention it. Actually, mention it. I want credit."

**5. SMALL.** Hal laughs through wet eyes.

**6. WIDE, bottom.** The three walk toward the school, Mel playing softly. Far behind them, Nikki glances back for half a second.
NIKKI'S FRIEND: "Nik?"
NIKKI: "Nothing."

---

## Page 9 (5 panels): Homeroom, meet Sir Archie
Layout: wide panel on top, small panel, medium panel, small panel, large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** Homeroom. Ceiling fans, chalk dust, open windows with a view of the courtyard.

**2. SMALL.** Sir Archie at the front. Calm man, tired smile, a coffee thermos on the desk. (Look is DRAFT.)
CAPTION: "Mr. Archibald Oronies. Everyone calls him Sir Archie."

**3. MEDIUM.** Roll call, quick cuts of each of the four.
SIR ARCHIE: "Victorio?"
JEFF: "Present, sir."
SIR ARCHIE: "Bautista?"
HAL: "Here, Sir! Alive!"
SIR ARCHIE: "Abaldado?" MEL: "Present."
SIR ARCHIE: "Legazpi?" NIKKI: "...Present."

**4. SMALL.** Seats: the three in a row, Nikki one row ahead. She turns to glare at them.

**5. LARGE.** Sir Archie writes on the board in big letters: SANGKAPULUAN.

---

## Page 10 (6 panels): The lesson
Layout: medium panel, wide illustrated panel, small panel, medium panel, small panel, small panel.

**1. MEDIUM.** Sir Archie.
SIR ARCHIE: "Before we start, a story. Long ago, our ancestors called these islands the Sangkapuluan."

**2. WIDE (illustrated, old ink map style).** The islands drawn like an ancient map. Ten small points glow along the map.
CAPTION: "Ten."

**3. SMALL.** Hal whispers.
HAL: "Ten what?"
JEFF (whispering back, eyes sparkling): "Agimats."

**4. MEDIUM.** Sir Archie.
SIR ARCHIE: "Every land has its Agimats. Charms with a power of their own. Ten are said to sleep in the Sangkapuluan, our islands."

**5. SMALL.** Nikki, bored.
NIKKI: "So it's a fairy tale, Sir?" The class laughs.
SIR ARCHIE: "Every fairy tale begins with someone who stopped listening."

**6. SMALL.** Jeff's hand drifts to his collar and closes around the necklace.
