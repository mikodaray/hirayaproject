# Chapter 1 Storyboard: Pages 11 to 20

Status: DRAFT. Part 2 of 5. Detention in the old storage room, the necklace reacts, the bamboo tube is found, and the reading begins.
Panel sizes: WIDE = full width strip, LARGE = about half the page, TALL = vertical strip, SMALL = about one sixth, INSET = tiny panel on top of another.

---

## Page 11 (5 panels): Detention
Layout: wide panel, three small panels, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** The bell. The class pours out.

**2. SMALL.** Hal, walking beside Jeff.
HAL: "Do you think Agimats are real?"

**3. SMALL.** Jeff, cool.
JEFF: "Statistically unlikely."
JEFF (smaller): "But I want them to be."

**4. SMALL.** Mel, shyly.
MEL: "I think music could be one."

**5. LARGE.** Sir Archie in the doorway, arms crossed. The four line up in front of him.
SIR ARCHIE: "I heard about the courtyard."
NIKKI: "He grabbed me!"
JEFF: "You swung first."
SIR ARCHIE: "Four sets of hands, one courtyard. The old storage room in the east building. All four of you. Today."

---

## Page 12 (5 panels): The east building
Layout: two small panels, a wide panel, a small panel, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. SMALL.** Nikki, scandalized.
NIKKI: "Sir! I have a reputation!"
SIR ARCHIE: "Then you'll finish faster."

**2. SMALL.** Mel, hopeful.
MEL: "Can I bring my violin?"
SIR ARCHIE: "Gently."

**3. WIDE.** Late afternoon. The four walk across the campus toward a far corner. Nikki walks behind the others, alone.

**4. SMALL.** Hal slows down and glances back at Nikki. She looks away quickly.

**5. LARGE.** The old east building: cracked paint, vines, a rusted padlock. Sir Archie hands Jeff a key.
SIR ARCHIE: "Dust only. Don't touch the cabinets at the back."
His eyes rest on Jeff's collar for half a second too long. Then he leaves.

---

## Page 13 (4 panels): The storage room
Layout: large panel on top, two small panels in the middle, a wide panel at the bottom.

**1. LARGE.** The door creaks open. Slats of light, floating dust, stacked old desks, cracked blackboards, boxes, a standing fan with a bent blade.

**2. SMALL.** Nikki, pinching her nose.
NIKKI: "It smells like grandma's closet."
HAL: "That's the best closet."

**3. SMALL.** Mel sneezes. A comic cloud of dust fills the panel.
SFX: ACHOOOO

**4. WIDE.** Jeff hands out gloves like a team captain.
JEFF: "Mel, top shelves. Hal, boxes. Nikki..."
NIKKI: "I'm not touching anything."
JEFF: "Great. You're supervisor."
She smirks. She likes that.

---

## Page 14 (6 panels): Cleaning, friendship beat
Layout: six panels in a grid, with the last one wider.

**1. SMALL.** Hal climbs a wobbling stack of boxes.

**2. SMALL.** Mel lifts three desks at once. Hal, from the floor: "Show off."

**3. SMALL.** A box slides. Hal tumbles into a cloud of dust. Nikki laughs, then stops when she hears him cough, and looks guilty for a second.

**4. SMALL.** Jeff finds an old textbook.
JEFF: "Whoa. A nineteen seventies Araling Panlipunan!"
HAL: "That's the nerdiest sentence you've ever said."

**5. SMALL.** Mel hums while sweeping. The dust floats in rhythm with the tune.

**6. WIDE.** Sunset through the window. The room is clean. All four laugh at something. Even Nikki smiles, until Hal looks at her, and she turns away.

---

## Page 15 (6 panels): The necklace reacts
Layout: small panel, close up panel, medium panel, two small panels, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. SMALL.** Jeff wipes his forehead. Under his collar, the necklace has a faint warm glow only the reader can see.

**2. CLOSE UP.** Jeff's hand closes around the pendant. His eyes widen. It is warm.

**3. MEDIUM.** He walks toward the back of the room. The glow grows brighter with every step.

**4. SMALL.** Hal.
HAL: "Jeff? You okay?"
JEFF: "Yeah. I just thought I heard something humming."

**5. SMALL.** Mel, head tilted.
MEL: "I hear it too. It's a low note. Like a B flat."

**6. LARGE.** All four turn to look at the same thing: the dark cabinet at the back of the room, the one Sir Archie warned about.

---

## Page 16 (5 panels): The cabinet
Layout: wide panel on top, two small panels, a medium panel, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** The cabinet: old dark wood, carved with faded Baybayin patterns, a rusted padlock.

**2. SMALL.** Nikki.
NIKKI: "Sir said don't touch it."
HAL: "That's what everyone says right before the horror movie."

**3. SMALL.** Jeff.
JEFF: "We're not breaking anything. Just looking."
MEL: "Looking is okay."

**4. MEDIUM.** Jeff steps close. The necklace flares. The rusted padlock crumbles to powder.
SFX: CLINK

**5. LARGE.** The doors swing open. Shelves of old school things, and in the middle a bamboo tube wrapped in faded cloth, covered in glowing carved script.

---

## Page 17 (6 panels): The tube
Layout: wide panel, close up panel, three small panels, a medium panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** The four gather in a half circle around the tube, now on a desk.

**2. CLOSE UP.** The carved script. Faint golden lines pulse inside the grooves.

**3. SMALL.** Hal.
HAL: "Is that... writing?"
JEFF: "Baybayin. The old script, before the Spanish. I've been studying it for fun."

**4. SMALL.** Nikki.
NIKKI: "Of course you have."

**5. SMALL.** Mel.
MEL: "The hum is coming from it."

**6. MEDIUM.** Hal tugging Jeff's sleeve.
HAL: "Maybe we should call Sir Archie?"
JEFF: "Just one line. I can read some of it."

---

## Page 18 (6 panels): Hiraya
Layout: wide panel, small panel, small panel, medium panel, two small panels.

**1. WIDE.** Jeff holds the tube. The others lean in. Orange sunlight on all four faces.

**2. SMALL.** His finger traces the first glyph.
JEFF: "Hi..."

**3. SMALL.** The second.
JEFF: "...ra..."

**4. MEDIUM.** Jeff.
JEFF: "Hiraya. It says hiraya manawari. It means, may your dreams come true."
NIKKI (quietly): "That's kind of nice."

**5. SMALL.** Hal.
HAL: "So it's a fortune cookie."

**6. SMALL.** Jeff, eyes scanning ahead.
JEFF: "There's more. A whole verse."

---

## Page 19 (6 panels): The dare
Layout: medium panel, two small panels, a medium panel, a small panel, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. MEDIUM.** All three look at Jeff.

**2. SMALL.** Nikki, arms crossed.
NIKKI: "Go on. Unless the weirdo's scared."

**3. SMALL.** Jeff.
JEFF: "I'm not scared. I'm thorough."

**4. MEDIUM.** Mel lifts his violin.
MEL: "I'll give it a tune. Every story needs a soundtrack."

**5. SMALL.** Hal grips a fistful of Jeff's shirt.
HAL: "If we die, I'm haunting all of you."

**6. LARGE.** Jeff inhales. Lifts the tube. Total silence. The glow in the grooves grows.

---

## Page 20 (6 panels): The reading
Layout: large panel on top, four small panels, a wide panel at the bottom.

**1. LARGE.** Jeff reads aloud. Ribbons of golden Baybayin script lift from the tube and curl around him.
CAPTION: "(Baybayin lines to be written and verified by a Baybayin source before print.)"

**2. SMALL.** Mel's violin joins, matching the rhythm of the words.

**3. SMALL.** Hal's glasses reflect gold light. His mouth is open.

**4. SMALL.** Nikki steps back. Her hair lifts in a wind that has no source.

**5. SMALL.** The necklace flares bright. The chain floats up off Jeff's neck.

**6. WIDE.** The whole room glows. A low hum swells.
SFX: HUUUUUUMMMMM
