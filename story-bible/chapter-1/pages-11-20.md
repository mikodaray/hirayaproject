# Chapter 1 Storyboard: Pages 11 to 20

Status: DRAFT, work in progress (new arc written 2026-11-12 from the creator's outline). The creator will add details a few at a time. Pages 1 to 10 are the approved introduction.
The old Butuan draft for these pages (detention, the bamboo tube, the fall through time) is saved in archive-butuan-draft/ and is no longer part of the story.
Panel sizes: WIDE = full width strip, LARGE = about half the page, TALL = vertical strip, SMALL = about one sixth, INSET = tiny panel on top of another.
Panels inside a page read left to right.
Dialogue is spoken in Bisaya, shown in English. Page counts, jokes and exact lines are DRAFT suggestions by Claude. The story beats are the creator's.

## Page 11 (5 panels): After school
Layout: wide panel, two small panels, a medium panel, a large panel at the bottom.
Why nobody is with Hal: DRAFT, TO CONFIRM. Jeff goes home early because his mother needs him, Mel has violin lessons, and Nikki's group has already left.

**1. WIDE.** The school gate in the golden afternoon light. The bell has rung. Students stream out. The three split at the gate.
JEFF: "I have to get home early. Mama needs me."
MEL: "Violin lesson. See you tomorrow."

**2. SMALL.** Hal, waving them off with a big grin.
HAL: "Go, go. I'm a grown man. I can walk home alone."

**3. SMALL.** Jeff and Mel glance back at him once, then leave.
SFX: "TAP TAP"

**4. MEDIUM.** Hal, alone, walking down the side path behind the school, humming and swinging his bag. The street is almost empty.

**5. LARGE.** A huge shadow falls over Hal. He turns and looks up, up, up. A very big senior student blocks the whole path.
CAPTION: "Dan Macabebe. Big. Loud. Allergic to being told no."
DAN: "Well, well. The pocket edition. Alone."

## Page 12 (5 panels): The bully
Layout: a large panel and a tall panel, two small panels, a wide panel at the bottom.

**1. LARGE.** Dan grabs Hal by the collar and lifts him off the ground. Hal's feet kick in the air.
HAL: "Put me DOWN!"
DAN: "Where's your bodyguard? Where's the weird one?"

**2. TALL.** Hal's face close up. Fear, but his jaw is set.
HAL: "I don't need a bodyguard!"

**3. SMALL.** Hal swings a wild punch. Dan holds him at arm's length with one hand, and the punch hits only air.
SFX: "WHIFF"

**4. SMALL.** Dan laughs and shoves Hal against the wall. Hal's books spill out of his bag.
SFX: "WHAM"

**5. WIDE.** Wide shot of the path. Dan towering over a small Hal pinned to the wall. A few students walk past in the background, look the other way, and keep walking. No one stops.
SFX: "THUD"
Art note: show the shoving and the cruelty, but nothing graphic. The point is that Hal is alone and nobody helps.

## Page 13 (4 panels): No one helps
Layout: two small panels, a wide panel, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. SMALL.** Dan stomps on one of Hal's notebooks and kicks his glasses away.
SFX: "CRUNCH"

**2. SMALL.** Hal on his hands and knees, patting the ground blindly for his glasses.

**3. WIDE.** Dan walks away with his arms behind his head, laughing. His laughter fills the empty street.
DAN: "Go home and cry to your mommy, shorty!"

**4. LARGE.** Hal alone on the ground, glasses back on, one lens cracked. He gathers his books slowly. His fists shake. His eyes are wet, but he refuses to let them fall.
SFX: "SHAKE SHAKE"

## Page 14 (5 panels): The walk home
Layout: wide panel, two small panels, a medium panel, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** Hal walks home along a quiet road at dusk, bag over his shoulder, muttering. The road is lined with old trees.
HAL: "Big dumb gorilla! Muscle-brained, banana-eating @#$%!"

**2. SMALL.** Hal, fuming, stomping, hands in fists.
HAL: "I'll show him. I'll learn karate. Tonight. In one night!"

**3. SMALL.** Hal's face flat, as he thinks it through.
HAL: "...No. I'll be dead by morning."

**4. MEDIUM.** Hal stops, spots a stone on the road, and picks it up. His hand trembles.

**5. LARGE.** Hal throws the stone as hard as he can, with a shout and a wild, angry swing. The stone flies toward the side of the road, toward a huge old balete tree.
HAL: "AAAAARGH!"
SFX: "FWOOSH"

## Page 15 (4 panels): The Agta
Layout: wide panel, two small panels, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** The stone hits the trunk of the old balete tree.
SFX: "TOK!"

**2. SMALL.** Hal, already walking away, dusting off his hands.
HAL: "Feels a little better."

**3. SMALL.** Behind him, the tree is still. Then the dark between the roots seems to breathe. A leaf falls. The wind stops. The air turns cold.
SFX: "...DOKI"

**4. LARGE.** High in the branches, two red eyes open. A long, thin, shadowy figure curls around the branch, a crooked grin full of teeth. It looks down at Hal's back as he walks away.
CAPTION: "The Agta. A hundred years of sleep, and a very bad mood."
AGTA: "Oh... what a delicious little hate."
Art note: DRAFT. The Agta is a mean spirited demon like spirit that preys on hatred and amplifies it. The design is a to do (see the todo list). Keep it a dark shape with red eyes for now.

## Page 16 (5 panels): Jeff comes home
Layout: wide panel, two small panels, two medium panels at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** A small house at evening, windows glowing. A jeepney is parked outside with its hood open. A painted slogan on the side reads GOD IS GOOD.
CAPTION: "Meanwhile."

**2. SMALL.** Jeff opens the door and steps in, bag on his shoulder.
JEFF: "I'm home."

**3. SMALL.** Inside the house: a small door bursts open, and two blurred shapes charge down the hall.
SFX: "PAT PAT PAT PAT"

**4. MEDIUM.** Two five year old twin girls leap at Jeff and grab one leg each.
CAPTION: "Gail and Janelle. Five years old. Professional huggers."
GAIL: "KUYA!"
JANELLE: "KUYA'S HOME!"

**5. MEDIUM.** Jeff with a twin on each leg like a pair of koalas, a flat annoyed stare, ears turning slightly red.
JEFF: "Get off. I just got home."
GAIL: "No."
JANELLE: "Never."

## Page 17 (5 panels): Two koalas
Layout: two small panels, a medium panel and a small panel, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. SMALL.** Jeff drags himself down the hall, a twin clinging to each leg. Deadpan.
SFX: "DRAG DRAG"

**2. SMALL.** Janelle holds up a drawing of a purple haired stick figure.
JANELLE: "I drew you, Kuya!"

**3. MEDIUM.** Jeff looks at the drawing. The stick figure has a huge head and a tiny body, and a banana hat.
JEFF: "Why do I have a banana on my head?"
JANELLE: "Because you're handsome."
JEFF: "...That doesn't make sense."

**4. SMALL.** Gail sticks a heart sticker on Jeff's cheek while he's distracted.

**5. LARGE.** Jeff tries to look annoyed, with a sticker on his cheek and a twin on each leg. His mouth fights a tiny smile and loses. Mama's voice calls from the kitchen.
MAMA (from the kitchen): "Girls! Let your Kuya breathe!"
GAIL AND JANELLE: "NO!"

## Page 18 (5 panels): Dinner with Mama
Layout: wide panel, two small panels, two medium panels at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** The family kitchen. A table with steaming rice and tinola. Mama, with an apron and a ladle in her hand, is at the stove.
CAPTION: "Mama. Runs the house like a general, with soup."

**2. SMALL.** Jeff sits and starts eating. The twins sit on either side of him.
SFX: "NOM NOM"

**3. SMALL.** Mama sets down a bowl and studies him closely.
MAMA: "So? How was school?"

**4. MEDIUM.** Jeff, mouth full.
JEFF: "Good. Nobody died."
MAMA: "That's your standard now?"
JEFF: "It's a high standard, Ma."

**5. MEDIUM.** Mama, with the ladle in one hand, narrows her eyes.
MAMA: "And Hal? Is he still eating enough? That poor little boy."
JEFF: "Ma, he's not little. He's compact."

## Page 19 (5 panels): Mama and the twins
Layout: two small panels, three wide panels stacked below.

**1. SMALL.** Mama slides a piece of fish onto Jeff's plate.
MAMA: "Eat more. You're too thin."

**2. SMALL.** Janelle's little hand sneaks across the table and steals the fish from Jeff's plate.
SFX: "SNATCH"

**3. WIDE.** Jeff, without even looking, takes the fish back from her fork. Janelle gasps. Gail gives her a thumbs up.
JEFF: "Nice try."
JANELLE: "...How?"
JEFF: "I have eyes in the back of my head."

**4. WIDE.** Mama, leaning on the table with her chin in her hand and a sly smile.
MAMA: "And any girlfriend I should know about?"

**5. WIDE.** Jeff chokes on his rice. The twins burst into giggles.
JEFF: "Ma! Eat your own rice!"
GAIL: "Kuya has a girlfriend!"
JANELLE: "Kuya has a banana girlfriend!"
SFX: "COUGH COUGH"

## Page 20 (5 panels): Papa
Layout: wide panel, two small panels, a large panel and a medium panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** The door at the back of the kitchen swings open. A tall man stands in the doorway, backlit by the yard light, a grease stained towel over his shoulder.
SFX: "CREAK"

**2. SMALL.** The man's face: tired eyes, a faint smile, grease smudged on his cheek. A small carved wooden charm hangs from a cord around his neck.
CAPTION: "Papa. Jeepney driver. Grease is his cologne."

**3. SMALL.** Papa, with a nod toward the yard.
PAPA: "Jeff. The jeepney is coughing again. Come help me."

**4. LARGE.** The yard. The jeepney sits with its hood open, a work lamp hanging over the engine. Jeff and Papa lean over the engine side by side, in the same posture, with the same frown.
SFX: "COUGH SPUTTER"

**5. MEDIUM.** Papa hands Jeff a wrench.
PAPA: "Hold this. No, the other one."
JEFF: "Pa, they're the same wrench."
PAPA: "The other one."
Art note: Hint only, not explained yet. Papa's old carved charm is a Babaylan symbol. The reveal that he was a Babaylan comes later. TO CONFIRM when.
