# Chapter 1 Storyboard: Pages 41 to 50

Status: DRAFT. Part 5 of 5. Guests of the Rajah, a quiet night, the elder's warning, and the chapter ending hook.
Panel sizes: WIDE = full width strip, LARGE = about half the page, TALL = vertical strip, SMALL = about one sixth, INSET = tiny panel on top of another.

---

## Page 41 (6 panels): Guests, maybe
Layout: wide panel, small panel, medium panel, two small panels, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** Uproar in the hall. The guards step forward again.

**2. SMALL.** The Rajah raises one hand. Silence.

**3. MEDIUM.** The Rajah.
RAJAH: [Bring them food and dry cloth. They are my guests until I say otherwise.]
JEFF: "We're guests. Maybe."

**4. SMALL.** Hal.
HAL: "That's the nicest way I've heard 'prisoner' said."

**5. SMALL.** The elder whispers to the Rajah. Both look at Jeff.

**6. LARGE.** Guards lead the four out. Jeff looks back. The elder's eyes follow him.

---

## Page 42 (6 panels): Borrowed clothes
Layout: wide panel, three small panels, a small panel, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** Evening. A guest house on stilts over the river. Torches, a gold sunset on the water.

**2. SMALL.** The four wear borrowed woven clothes. Hal's is far too big.
HAL: "I look like a very sad tablecloth."

**3. SMALL.** Mel holds up a tiny wrap, deeply sorry.
MEL: "This will not cover me."
A servant hurries in with a bigger cloth.

**4. SMALL.** Nikki steps out in a woven skirt, hair tied up. The guys stare.
NIKKI: "WHAT."

**5. SMALL.** Jeff, quickly looking away.
JEFF: "Nothing. You look... ready."

**6. LARGE.** The four sit on the floor around grilled fish, rice, and fruit. Steam. Soft light. For a moment they just eat.

---

## Page 43 (6 panels): Home
Layout: wide panel, three small panels, a medium panel, a small panel.

**1. WIDE.** The four sitting at the edge of the veranda. Lanterns float on the river below.

**2. SMALL.** Hal.
HAL: "So we just read it again and go home, right?"

**3. SMALL.** Jeff.
JEFF: "The tube. Did anyone grab it?"

**4. SMALL.** Mel shakes his head slowly. Nikki too.
JEFF: "It's gone. It was gone the second we landed."

**5. MEDIUM.** Hal's voice cracks.
HAL: "I want to go home. I want my mom."
Nikki puts a stiff hand on his shoulder.
NIKKI: "Don't cry. It's annoying."
She does not take her hand away.

**6. SMALL.** Jeff watches them. He smiles a little.
JEFF: "We'll find a way. Together."

---

## Page 44 (5 panels): The necklace
Layout: large panel on top, two small panels, a medium panel, a wide panel at the bottom.

**1. LARGE.** Night. Jeff alone at the veranda edge. The river reflects stars, far more of them than at home.

**2. SMALL (flashback, soft borders).** A small box opened on a birthday. A man's hands, his face in shadow, putting a necklace in Jeff's palm.
FATHER: "Keep this close, anak. Never take it off."
(The father's name, look, and what he knows are all OPEN.)

**3. SMALL.** Jeff, thought:
CAPTION: "Dad. What did you give me?"

**4. MEDIUM.** Behind him, footsteps. The elder advisor with a lantern.

**5. WIDE.** The elder, quietly.
ELDER: [Who gave you that?]
JEFF: "My father."
ELDER: [Then he knew what you carry. Do you?]

## Page 45 (6 panels): The warning
Layout: medium panel, small panel, small panel, small panel, a large panel, a small panel.

**1. MEDIUM.** Jeff.
JEFF: "No."
ELDER: [Then it woke in my land for a reason.]

**2. SMALL.** The elder.
ELDER: [The Sangkapuluan keep ten. Not all of them sleep. Not all of them are friends.]

**3. SMALL.** Jeff.
JEFF: "How do I get home?"

**4. SMALL.** The elder.
ELDER: [The door you opened swings one way, child. Until a reason opens it back.]

**5. LARGE.** The elder turns to go, his lantern swaying.
ELDER: [Tomorrow, the Rajah will ask something of you.]

**6. SMALL.** Jeff, alone with the lantern light, hand closed around the necklace.

---

## Page 46 (6 panels): Morning
Layout: wide panel, three small panels, a small panel, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** Sunrise on the river. Hal asleep with his arms and legs spread out. Mel curled around the violin. Nikki awake, sitting at the edge, hair loose.

**2. SMALL.** Jeff sits down near her.
NIKKI: "Don't say anything."

**3. SMALL.** Nikki.
NIKKI: "I'm still not your friend."
JEFF: "Never said you were."

**4. SMALL.** Nikki, very quietly.
NIKKI: "Thanks. For yesterday. For Hal's glasses."
JEFF: "Tell him."
NIKKI: "Never."

**5. SMALL.** Hal, eyes still closed, rolls over.
HAL: "I can hear you."

**6. LARGE.** Nikki turns bright red. SFX in the background: "!!!" Jeff and Mel, who was awake all along, burst out laughing.

## Page 47 (5 panels): Summoned
Layout: wide panel, two small panels, a medium panel, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** A messenger arrives at the guest house door with two guards.

**2. SMALL.** Messenger.
MESSENGER: [The Rajah calls the boy with the bright hair.]

**3. SMALL.** Jeff.
JEFF: "He means me. The purple one."
HAL: "You're the bright hair."

**4. MEDIUM.** Jeff tucks the necklace into his wrap. Hal grabs his sleeve.
HAL: "We go together."
MEL: "Together."
NIKKI: "...Fine."

**5. LARGE.** The four walk side by side toward the hall, backlit by the morning sun.

## Page 48 (5 panels): The demand
Layout: wide panel, medium panel, two small panels, a large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** The hall again. Warriors line the walls. The elder stands to the Rajah's side, his face unreadable.

**2. MEDIUM.** The Rajah, from his seat.
RAJAH: [Boy. Give me what hangs on your neck.]

**3. SMALL.** The court is silent. Warriors tighten their grip on their spears.

**4. SMALL.** Jeff does not move.

**5. LARGE.** The Rajah rises, one step down from the dais.
RAJAH: [It is not a request.]

**2. SMALL.** Mel.

JEFF: "Dili, Rajah."

## Page 50 (3 panels): Chapter ending hook
Layout: a wide panel on top, a medium close up in the middle, a very large panel at the bottom.

**1. WIDE.** The hall explodes into motion. Spears rise. The Rajah's eyes narrow.
