# Creation stories used as the base of the mythos

Source: https://sites.pitt.edu/~dash/creation-phil.html (a page of Philippine creation stories). Summaries below follow that page. Names are spelled the way the page spells them.
Rule: we use the shapes and images of these stories as the base, and we change them for Hiraya. Every change is marked as our own.

Warning about the source: two of these stories (1 and 8) end by explaining the skin colors and social classes of peoples. Do not use those parts. They are outdated, unfair, and do not fit this story. We only use the beginnings of those stories.

## The stories

### 1. How the World Was Made (a general ancient Filipino account, no group named)
Only sea and sky at first. Maguayan ruled the sea and Captan ruled the sky. Their children Lidagat (sea) and Lihangin (wind) had four children: Licalibutan, Liadlao, Libulan, and Lisuga. Proud Licalibutan led his brothers to attack Captan's sky. Captan struck them with lightning. Libulan became the moon, Liadlao the sun, Lisuga's silver body scattered into the stars, and Licalibutan's rocky body broke apart to become land. Maguayan planted a seed on that land, and from a bamboo tree came the first people, Sicalac and Sicabay.

### 2. The Creation (Igorot)
The Great Spirit Lumawig cut reeds and made pairs of people. Each pair spoke a different language. He then gave each place its own skill, and moved a skill to another place when people failed to use it.

### 3. How the Moon and the Stars Came to Be (Bukidnon, Mindanao)
The sky used to be close to the ground. A woman pounding rice hung her comb and beads on the sky. Her pestle struck the sky so hard it rose far away, leaving her comb to become the moon and her beads the stars.

### 4. Origin (Bagobo, Mindanao)
The first couple, Toglai and Toglibon, lived with their children at Cibolan on Mount Apo. A three year drought scattered the children. Each pair took something from Cibolan, and what they carried gave their descendants their names. The last two children, a boy and a girl, were too weak to leave. The boy found a sugarcane stalk whose juice saved them, and the Bagobo come from them.

### 5. The Story of the Creation (Bilaan, Mindanao)
Melu, a giant of the clouds, rubbed dead skin from his body into a pile and made the earth, then made two small people. Tau Tana, a being from under the earth, joined in and put their noses on upside down. A great rain came, and Melu came back and fixed the noses. The people promised to serve him, and he told them to save their hair and dry skin so he could make companions for them.

### 6. In the Beginning (Bilaan, Mindanao)
Four beings, Melu, Fiuweigh, Diwata, and Saweigh, lived on an island no bigger than a hat with a bird named Buswit. Buswit fetched soil, rattan, and fruit. Melu shaped the soil into the earth and planted the seeds, which became forests. The first people made of wax melted in the fire, so they tried clay. A companion put the noses on upside down, and Melu secretly fixed them, which left a fingerprint mark on people's faces.

### 7. The Children of the Limokon (Mandaya, Mindanao)
Before people, the limokon, a dove like bird that could speak, laid two eggs, one near the mouth of the Mayo River and one farther upstream. They hatched into a man and a woman. The lonely man saw a hair float against his legs and followed it upstream until he found the woman. They married, and their children became the Mandaya people.

### 8. The Creation Story (Tagalog)
Only sea and sky, with a kite bird between them. The tired kite stirred the sea until it struck the sky, and the sky dropped islands to hold the sea back. The land breeze and sea breeze had a bamboo child. The bamboo drifted to shore and the angry kite pecked it open, and a man and a woman came out. Their children scattered to different hiding places.

## What each story gives Hiraya (our own adaptations, all DRAFT)

| Story | Image or idea | How we can use it |
|-------|---------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Bamboo gives the first people | The bamboo tube is a piece of the first bamboo, and carries the first words |
| 1 | Licalibutan's body breaks into land, Lisuga into stars | The islands (the Sangkapuluan) are the broken body of the proud one, which is where the ten Agimats rest |
| 1 | Licalibutan's pride and Captan's lightning | The source of the dangerous Agimats, born from pride and wishing for power |
| 1 | Maguayan plants a seed after the destruction | The seed is the First Wish, a hope that the broken world will live again. The source says only that he planted a seed, the meaning is our addition |
| 2 | Pairs of people, each with their own language | Why Butuan speaks an older Bisaya, and why hearing each other is hard |
| 3 | The sky was once close to the ground | In the first age words could reach the sky. Reading Baybayin aloud briefly makes the sky close again |
| 4 | Each pair carried something from the first home, and it named their people | The ten Agimats are ten things carried away from the first home when the people scattered |
| 4 | Cibolan on Mount Apo in Mindanao | A real, nearby mountain that can be the first home, close to Durian City and to Butuan's region |
| 4 | Two weak children saved by sugarcane | Hope in hunger. The weakest ones can carry the most important thing |
| 7 | A speaking bird begins the people | A bird that speaks is the first voice. Fits reading aloud, and gives us a possible bird spirit or messenger |
| 7 | A man follows a floating hair upstream to find the woman | A thread that leads you to the person you need, like the necklace pulling Jeff to the cabinet |
| 5 and 6 | A maker's secret fix leaves a fingerprint on every face | Every person carries a trace of the maker, a possible detail for how an Agimat knows its bearer |
| 8 | A kite bird stirs sea against sky | A first mover that starts everything, possible extra spirit |

## Story facts we already use that match these stories
- Butuan sits on the Agusan River in Mindanao, and Mount Apo and the Mayo River are in the same island, so the creation stories of Mindanao are close to the setting.
- Bamboo is in the premise already (the tube), and bamboo is the first people's cradle in stories 1 and 8, and reeds in story 2.
