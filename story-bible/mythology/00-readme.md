# Mythology of Hiraya

This folder holds the main mythos used for the whole story, across every chapter and arc. Anything about Agimats, spirits, Baybayin as a power, and the Sangkapuluan goes here first, then chapter files use it.

Marking rules, same as the rest of the story bible:
* DECIDED means the creator confirmed it.
* DRAFT means it is a suggestion from Claude, not confirmed.
* OPEN means not decided yet.
* VERIFIED or TO CHECK for real folklore and history facts.

Status: first draft started 2026-11-10. Only the facts marked DECIDED are locked.

## Files
- 01-core-truths.md: the rules of the mythos, what is decided and what is a draft
- 02-the-ten-agimats.md: the ten Agimats, by patron god and rank
- 07-beings-and-demigods.md: the children of the gods who became creatures, beings, and demigods
- 06-the-gods-and-creation.md: the main creation story (decided), Maguayan and Captan, and their descendants
- 05-creation-stories.md: the creation stories the mythos is based on, and how each one is used
- 03-folklore-sources.md: real Philippine folklore we can draw from, with sources
- 04-open-questions.md: what the creator needs to decide next

## How to use this folder
When a chapter needs a power, a spirit, or a rule of magic, check here first. If it is not here, add it here as DRAFT, then confirm it with the creator before the chapter relies on it.
