# Bestiary by lineage

Every folklore creature from the Wikipedia list of Philippine mythological creatures (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Philippine_mythological_creatures), sorted under the god whose line it belongs to. The descriptions follow that page. Status: DRAFT. The sorting is my proposal and the creator can move anything.

## How I sorted (rules)
1. Domain first. A sea creature goes to the sea lines, a sky bird to Captan, a giant of stone to Licalibutan.
2. Stated myth second. If folklore already ties a being to a god or a place (such as Sinogo, the messenger of Kaptan), that tie decides.
3. Behavior third. Beings that prey on people, spread disease, or turn on others are the children of pride and go to Licalibutan's line, with the night fliers going to Libulan.
4. Where folklore is a group name (aswang, engkanto, diwata), the group is placed once, and the notes say where its subtypes could split.
5. Roles used in the tables: C is a creature, B is a being (thinking, speaking, with a will), D is a demigod candidate, a being already so strong in folklore that it could be a demigod with powers.

Regions are the ones the source page gives. "Not stated" means the page does not say.

## A note on the diwata
The Anito article (Wikipedia) says diwata are non human spirits tied to places, objects, plants, animals, or natural events, and that most are kind or neutral. In this universe each lineage has its own bound diwata:
- Maguayan and Lidagat: water and sea diwata, springs and rivers.
- Captan: sky, storm, and mountain peak diwata.
- Lihangin: diwata of winds and air.
- Licalibutan: diwata of land, caves, mountains, and trees. These are the acacia and balete tree diwata in the folklore.
- Liadlao, Libulan, Lisuga: diwata of sun, moon, and stars.
- Sicalac and Sicabay: house and family diwata.

## Gaps and ideas (DRAFT)
- Liadlao, Libulan, and Lisuga have few creatures. They need new beings or the creator's own inventions.
- Libulan has no gentle night beings in folklore. A good invention: dream guardians who fight the night fliers.
- Maguayan has many strong deep creatures but few friendly ones. The Sama Stingray is a good one.
- Several beings sit between two lines (Intumbangel, Gisurab, Tamawo). Those cross lines can be used for conflict or marriage between lineages.
- The Swallowers (Bakunawa, Sawa, Olimaw, Arimaonga, Samal Naga, Minokawa) hunger for the sun and moon. This is a possible large threat across the story.
