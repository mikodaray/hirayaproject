# Jeff's family home (Victorio house)

Status: LOCKED LOOK. Reference image: art/places/jeff-family-home-v1.png (made by the creator, 2026-11-12). Use it as the image reference for every shot of Jeff's home, inside or out.
Black and white manga style in the reference. For color pages, keep the same shapes and materials.

## What it is
- The home of Jeff, Mama, Papa, the younger twins Gail and Janelle, and the older sister mentioned later.
- A two storey house with an open carport on the ground floor. Papa's jeepney is parked in the carport, so it is also his workplace and the place where he and Jeff fix the jeepney (pages 20 to 22).
- Simple but well kept and proud. A working family home, not rich, not poor. A mix of modern concrete and old Filipino wood and woven bamboo, which also fits Papa's Babaylan past.

## Exterior, from the image
- Ground floor: plain concrete. Open carport on the left with a flat concrete ceiling, strip light and wooden beams. Cabinet and shelf of tools and boxes at the back wall of the carport. Wide concrete driveway. Concrete pillars hold up the upper floor.
- Front door: big double wooden door with carved panels, under the porch, with a hanging lantern above it. Three wide concrete steps. Big pots of plants on both sides, and a small plant box beside the steps.
- Wall lamps on the pillars next to the door.
- Upper floor: dark timber frame with woven bamboo walls (sawali or amakan, a basket weave pattern). Wooden louvered windows (capiz style shutters), one large window on the left, double wooden doors opening to the balcony.
- Balcony: runs across the front of the upper floor and turns the corner on the right. Wooden balusters and rails. Hanging lantern, hanging wind chime, small potted plants.
- Roof: tall steep gable roof with small clay tiles, wide eaves with exposed wooden rafters and brackets, a small roof over the balcony in the same tile. A small vent panel in the gable.
- Gate and fence: concrete gate pillar on the right with a square plaque showing a sun emblem (eight rays, like the Philippine sun) and "EST. 2020", wooden slat fence beside it. Metal sliding or swing gate on the left of the carport.
- Surroundings: tall coconut palm on the right behind the house, banana plants and tropical leaves, a power pole with lines, green hills behind and to the right. Dappled tree shadows on the driveway.
- Light: bright afternoon sun, high white clouds.

## The jeepney (parked in the carport)
- Classic Philippine jeepney, chrome grille and bumper, round headlights, ornate side details, passenger windows along the side.
- Sign above the windshield: BIYAHE TAYO (suggested name for Papa's jeepney, to be confirmed).
