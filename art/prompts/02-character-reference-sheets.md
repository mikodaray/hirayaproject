# Character reference sheet prompts

Use these to generate one reference image per character. Save each result in art/characters/ as NN-name-sheet-vN.png and attach it as a reference image in future generations so the faces stay the same.

Status: DRAFT. Character sheets are in full color (the creator wants color for these, interior chapter pages stay black and white). Looks marked (DRAFT) are my suggestions, not decided by the creator.

## How to use
1. Paste the COMMON STYLE block first, then the character block.
2. Generate the main sheet. If the face is wrong, regenerate before moving on.
3. Once you like one, attach it as a reference and generate the expression sheet.
4. Save both in art/characters/ and note the version in art/README.md.

## COMMON STYLE (paste before every character)
Manga character reference sheet in full color, hand drawn with bold ink pen linework and flat cel style coloring with simple shading, in the style of a classic shonen manga like Naruto: bold confident line, varied line weight, expressive faces. Not realistic, not 3D, not painted. Plain white background. Full body character shown three times in a row: front view, side view, back view, all standing straight at the same height. Next to them a large close up of the face and a row of small head studies showing neutral, laughing, angry, surprised, and embarrassed expressions. Small arrows pointing to key details. No readable text, no labels, no logos. Setting of the story: modern Philippines, Durian Public High School.
Use the exact colors given in each character block.

## 1. Jeff (Jefferson Victorio), main protagonist
Jefferson "Jeff" Victorio, 19 years old, tall and lean, 5 foot 10, the cool guy who is also a little nerdy. Ube purple hair (a soft violet purple like ube), messy and a bit spiky, falling over one eye. Relaxed half lidded confident eyes, a small calm smirk. Sharp but friendly face. School uniform: blue short sleeved buttoned shirt (school blue) with the top button open, khaki tan pants, plain black school shoes, a thin backpack strap on one shoulder. Under the collar he wears a small pendant necklace on a chain, a simple carved stone charm, drawn with a tiny glint. Standing pose: one hand in pocket, relaxed, effortless. Height reference: he is the second tallest of the four friends.
Expression row: calm smirk, nerd excitement with sparkling eyes, serious protective glare, mild surprise, small shy smile.

Hal Bautista, teenage boy, exactly 5 foot flat, very short and lean, looks cute, the shortest of the four. ## 3. Mel (Mel Charlie Abaldado), the musical genius
Mel Charlie Abaldado, teenage boy, 6 foot 2, big and on the heavy side, but gentle looking with soft rounded features and a kind face. Long golden blonde hair past the shoulders, calm closed or soft eyes. School uniform: blue short sleeved buttoned shirt, khaki pants. He always carries a violin, with the bow, in the standing view tucked under his arm and in one view played under his chin. He is the tallest and widest of the four.
