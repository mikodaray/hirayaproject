# Prompts with dialogue: Chapter 1, Pages 1 to 50

Status: DRAFT. Built from the storyboard in story-bible/chapter-1/. Every page prompt now includes the dialogue, captions, and sound effects so the generator can letter them.
Aspect ratio: 2:3 portrait. Black and white, hand drawn, read left to right (all DECIDED, changed 2026-11-08).
Attach as references: the character sheets in art/characters/ (see prompts/02-character-reference-sheets.md) and one approved page for the style.

## How to use
1. Paste the COMMON PREFIX, then the page block, then the COMMON ENDING.
2. Image generators often misspell lettering. If a bubble comes out wrong, regenerate, or generate the page with the bubbles empty (delete the "Lettering" lines) and add the text later. The viewer in index.html can also draw the text on top (lettering.js).
3. Lines in [square brackets] are people of Butuan speaking their older Bisaya. Draw them in the same bubbles, but you can keep the brackets off in the final art.
4. Quoted text must be copied exactly. If the generator keeps failing on long lines, shorten the line in the storyboard and this prompt file together.

## COMMON PREFIX
A single vertical black and white manga page, aspect ratio 2:3, made to be read from left to right, top to bottom, with clean white gutters between panels. Hand drawn manga illustration in black ink on white paper, bold varied ink linework, hatching, and gray screentone dot shading. Strictly black, white, and gray only, no color. It must clearly look like a drawing, not realistic, not 3D, not photographic. Bold shonen manga style close to classic Naruto manga artwork, expressive faces and dynamic perspective. Setting: Durian City High School in Durian City, a modern Makati like Philippine city. Characters, same designs as the reference image but in black and white: Jeff is a tall lean 19 year old with messy hair in mid gray screentone, calm and cool; Hal is an 18 year old who is very short, about 5 feet, lean, with young adult proportions (not childlike), neatly combed brown hair in light gray screentone, round glasses, cute; Mel is very tall and big, soft and gentle looking, with long blonde hair in light screentone, and always carries a violin; Nikki is a pretty, popular girl with long brown hair, curvy figure, and a comedian streak. Boys wear a short sleeved buttoned shirt and khaki pants, girls wear a white short sleeved blouse and a white skirt. Panels read left to right, top to bottom.

## COMMON ENDING
Draw speech bubbles, caption boxes, and sound effect lettering exactly as listed in each panel, spelled correctly, in a clean hand lettered comic style. Place each bubble inside or next to its panel without covering the speaker's face. Tails of the speech bubbles point to the speaker. Reading order of bubbles inside a panel is left to right, top to bottom. Do not add any other text, no extra words, no watermark, no logo. Anatomy must be correct: every person has exactly two arms and exactly two hands, each hand has exactly five fingers (four fingers and one thumb), and exactly two legs, with no extra, missing, merged, or duplicated arms, hands, fingers, or legs. Count the limbs and fingers of every person, including people in the background. Avoid color, realistic or photographic rendering, 3D rendering, extra or missing fingers, more than five fingers on a hand, more than two arms, a third arm, extra or duplicate arms, hands, or legs, merged limbs, deformed hands, blurry results, ninja outfits, headbands, and copies of any existing characters.

## Page 2 (5 panels): Meet Hal
Layout: wide strip on top, two small panels, then a tall panel on the left and a large panel on the right. 

Panel 1 (wide, top): Behind Jeff, a tiny figure sprints through the crowd, a backpack bigger than he is. Lettering: sound effect lettering reads "TAP TAP TAP TAP"

Panel 2 (small): Jeff does not turn around. Small smirk. Lettering: speech bubble from Jeff reads "Three. Two. One."

Panel 3 (small): Hal crashes into Jeff's back. Glasses slide down his nose. Lettering: sound effect lettering reads "THUD"; speech bubble from Hal reads "JEFF! Wait up!"

Panel 4 (tall, left): Hal's face close up. Brown hair, round glasses, big eyes. He is very cute and very offended. Lettering: speech bubble from Hal reads "You walked past the bakery again! I texted you six times!"

Panel 5 (large, right): The height gap. Jeff looks down, Hal looks up, a paper bag of pandesal held high like a trophy. Lettering: speech bubble from Hal reads "I got your favorite."; speech bubble from Jeff reads "You're a legend."; caption box reads "Hal Bautista. Loudest voice in any room."

## Page 3 (6 panels): Meet Mel
Layout: wide strip on top, a tall panel on the left, three stacked small panels in the middle, a large panel at the bottom. 

Panel 1 (wide, top): Violin music drifts across the school grounds. Students stop on the stairs. Glowing musical notes weave through the air.

Panel 2 (tall, left): Mel on the school steps, eyes closed, long blonde hair over his shoulder, violin tucked under his chin. A very big, very gentle guy.

Panel 3 (small): Jeff and Hal arrive at the bottom of the steps. Hal waves with the pandesal bag.

Panel 4 (small): Mel opens his eyes and smiles without stopping the music.

Panel 5 (small): Mel's bow slows, then stops. Lettering: speech bubble from Mel reads "Good morning. I made something new."

Panel 6 (large, bottom): The trio together. Hal has climbed two steps to reach Mel's shoulder height. Jeff leans on the railing with his book. Lettering: speech bubble from Jeff reads "It sounds like sunrise."; speech bubble from Hal reads "It sounds like pandesal."; speech bubble from Mel reads "Both are correct."; caption box reads "Mel Charlie Abaldado. The violin is the one thing that always makes sense to him."

## Page 4 (5 panels): Meet Nikki
Layout: wide strip on top, a small panel and a tall panel, a large low angle panel in the middle, a medium panel at the bottom. 

Panel 1 (wide, top): The gate opens. Wind. A group of girls in white blouses and skirts. No dialogue.

Panel 2 (small): Hal freezes mid bite. Lettering: speech bubble from Hal reads "..No."

Panel 3 (tall): Students step aside, one after another, like a slow wave.

Panel 4 (large low angle): Nikki walks forward, hair in the wind, her friends flanking her. She owns the sidewalk. Lettering: caption box reads "Nikki Sharon Legazpi. The most popular girl in school."

Panel 5 (medium, bottom): She stops in front of the trio. Smug. Lettering: speech bubble from Nikki reads "Look. The nerd, the giant, and the pocket edition."

## Page 5 (6 panels): Nikki picks on Hal

Panel 1 (small): Hal bristles, finger raised. Lettering: speech bubble from Hal reads "I am NOT a pocket edition!"

Panel 2 (wide): Nikki plucks his glasses off his face and holds them high. Her friends laugh.

Panel 3 (small): Hal's blurry point of view: smears of color and shapes. Lettering: thought bubble from Hal reads "I can't see anything"

Panel 6 (medium, bottom): Jeff calm. His necklace swings once against his chest.

## Page 6 (5 panels): Jeff steps in
Layout: wide panel, two medium panels, a small panel, and a large panel at the bottom that ends on a cliffhanger. 

Panel 1 (wide): Jeff steps between Nikki and Hal. We see his back. Hal peeks out behind his arm.

Panel 2 (medium): Jeff, steady. Lettering: speech bubble from Jeff reads "Give them back."; speech bubble from Nikki reads "Make me."

Panel 3 (medium): Jeff's smirk. Lettering: speech bubble from Jeff reads "Do you ever get tired of being this predictable?"; speech bubble from Nikki reads "Do you ever get tired of reading about ghosts?"

Panel 4 (small): Hal, from behind Jeff. Lettering: speech bubble from Hal reads "They're not ghosts. It's folklore!"; speech bubble from Mel reads "It's technically mythology."

Panel 5 (large): Nikki, smug and annoyed, flings Hal's glasses high over Jeff's head in a big arc, her arm swung out in a showy throw, with speed lines and a glint on the lenses. Her friends gasp. Lettering: speech bubble from Nikki reads "Fine. Go fetch."; sound effect lettering reads "FLING"

## Page 7 (full page splash): Jeff proves the point
Layout: Different layout: a single full page splash, one image, no panels, no gutters. 

Panel 1 (full page): Jeff, without even turning around, reaches up and catches the glasses between two fingers, calm and stylish. Dramatic angle from slightly below. Behind him Hal's eyes are huge, Mel's bow is frozen mid air, the students are frozen. Nikki's friends have their hands over their mouths and small hearts in their eyes. Leaves and dust float in a shaft of morning sun. Nikki's face is shocked. Lettering: speech bubble from Jeff reads "Careful. Those are his."; caption box reads "Jeff Victorio never starts a fight. He just never lets his friends stand alone."

Art note: this is the page that shows Jeff is more than the weird folklore kid. Everyone else in the frame is in comic shock, Jeff is the only calm face. He looks cool, and for one moment the school sees it. Nikki's girls fall for him on the spot.
