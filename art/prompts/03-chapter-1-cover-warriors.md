## Prompt
```
A vertical manga chapter cover illustration, aspect ratio 2:3, in full color, hand drawn with bold ink linework, flat cel style coloring with simple shading, in the style of a classic shonen manga cover like Naruto. It must clearly look like a drawing, not realistic, not 3D, not photographic.

Reference images: the first attached image is the HIRAYA logo. The next four are the character sheets of Jeff, Hal, Mel and Nikki. Draw each character exactly as on their sheet: same face, hair, skin tone and body type. Use the sheets for the look only, do not copy the sheet poses or the sheet expressions. Each face must be animated and show a clear emotion that fits this moment, with the head turned and the eyes looking where described below. Dress them in ancient Filipino warrior costumes from around the year 1000 instead of school uniforms.

Composition: a dramatic low angle wide lens group shot, as the four step out of a glowing mist onto the river port of ancient Butuan. Jeff is in the front center, close to the camera. The other three are arranged around and behind him on a diagonal. Sizes: Mel is the tallest and biggest, Jeff is the second tallest, Nikki is shorter, and Hal is the shortest and his head only reaches Jeff's chest.

Expressions and poses:
Jeff: fierce and determined, a bold grin, both eyes visible and locked on the viewer, head slightly lowered, a long curved kampilan sword resting on his shoulder with one hand, his small stone pendant glowing on his chest. He wears a sleeveless dark woven vest and woven wrap, gold armbands and leather wrist guards.
Hal: terrified and thrilled at the same time, eyes wide, mouth open in a shout, looking up at the giant creatures behind them, holding a small round wooden shield (kalasag) and a bamboo staff with a bamboo tube tied on it. He wears a woven wrap and small gold earrings.
Mel: serene and lost in the music, eyes closed, a soft smile, head tilted toward his wooden violin, golden musical notes swirling from it into the sky. He wears a woven sleeveless top and a wide woven wrap with gold trim and a gold headband.
Nikki: laughing out loud with her head thrown back a little, one eye winking at the viewer, a hand on her hip, a bolo blade at her waist. She wears a woven wrap skirt with bright patterns and a short fitted woven top, with gold earrings and a gold necklace, a modest warrior outfit.

Background: a sunrise over the river port of Butuan, wooden plank boats with striped sails and stilt houses, with golden light. Rising behind the four, large and dramatic but not too scary, are Filipino folklore creatures: a giant bakunawa sea serpent coiling across the sky around a pale moon, a tikbalang with a horse head and long legs standing in the mist on the right, a tall kapre sitting in a big tree on the left, a diwata nature spirit with soft glowing butterfly wings floating near the top, a colorful sarimanok bird with long tail feathers flying above, and a faint flying manananggal silhouette in the clouds. Golden ribbons of Baybayin style script curl through the air around the characters, with no readable words.

Text on the cover: at the top, use the attached HIRAYA logo exactly as it is, the same gold letters, the same outline and the same sun symbol, only scaled to fit, do not redraw or restyle it and keep it spelled H I R A Y A. Directly under the logo, a smaller subtitle line reading exactly Chapter 1: Banaag. At the bottom left corner, small neat credit text reading exactly Authored by Claude and Miko Daray. No other text.

Anatomy must be correct: every person has exactly two arms and exactly two hands, each hand has exactly five fingers (four fingers and one thumb), and exactly two legs, with no extra, missing, merged, or duplicated arms, hands, fingers, or legs. Count the limbs and fingers of every person, including people in the background. Avoid school uniforms, the same expression on every character, realistic or photographic rendering, 3D rendering, extra or missing fingers, more than five fingers on a hand, more than two arms, a third arm, extra or duplicate arms, hands, or legs, merged limbs, deformed hands, blurry results, ninja outfits, headbands that look like ninja headbands, scary gore, a redrawn or changed logo, misspelled text, and copies of any existing characters.
```
