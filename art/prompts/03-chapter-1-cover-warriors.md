# Chapter 1 cover prompt, version 2: warrior costumes and folklore creatures

Status: DRAFT. Made 2026-11-06 at the creator's request. The creator wants all main characters in ancient Filipino warrior costumes with Filipino folklore creatures in the background.
Aspect ratio: 2:3 portrait. Covers stay in full color (see art/ART-STYLE.md).
Attach as references: the character sheets for Jeff, Hal, Mel and Nikki from art/characters/. Keep their faces, hair and body sizes, but use the costumes below instead of school uniforms.
Costume and creature notes are DRAFT flavor. Costumes lean on Visayan and Mindanao warriors of around the year 1000 (woven wraps, gold ornaments, shields, blades). Creatures are chosen from Visayan and Mindanao folklore. See story-bible/05-research-notes.md.

## Prompt
```
A vertical manga chapter cover illustration, aspect ratio 2:3, in full color, hand drawn with bold ink linework, expressive faces, and flat cel style coloring with simple shading, in the style of a classic shonen manga cover like Naruto. It must clearly look like a drawing, not realistic, not 3D, not photographic. Use the attached character sheets for the faces, hair and body types of the four main characters, but dress them in ancient Filipino warrior costumes from around the year 1000 instead of school uniforms.

Composition: a dramatic low angle wide lens group shot. Jeff stands in the front center, close to the camera, calm and cool. The other three are arranged around and behind him on a diagonal, animated and funny. Sizes: Mel is the tallest and biggest, Jeff is the second tallest, Nikki is shorter, and Hal is the shortest and his head only reaches Jeff's chest.

The four characters:
Jeff, tall and lean, messy ube purple hair falling over one eye, a calm confident smirk. He wears a warrior outfit of a sleeveless dark woven vest and a woven loincloth wrap over short trousers, gold armbands, leather wrist guards, and bare feet or simple sandals. He holds a long curved kampilan sword resting on his shoulder with one hand, and the small carved stone pendant necklace glows softly on his chest.
Hal, short and slim with neat combed brown hair and round glasses, a startled and excited face. He wears a woven wrap and small gold earrings, and holds a small round wooden shield (kalasag) and a bamboo staff with a bamboo tube tied on it, the glasses catching the light. He is clearly the funny, nervous one.
Mel, big, tall and gentle, with long golden blonde hair. He wears a woven sleeveless top and a wide woven wrap with gold trim and a gold headband. He plays his wooden violin with his eyes closed, and glowing golden musical notes swirl out from it into the sky.
Nikki, with long wavy brown hair, a confident smirk and a hand on her hip. She wears a woven wrap skirt with bright patterns, a short fitted woven top, gold earrings and a gold necklace, and a bolo blade at her waist, in a modest warrior outfit. She looks proud and a little cocky, like she is about to tell a joke.

Background: a sunrise over the river port of Butuan, wooden plank boats with striped sails and stilt houses, with golden light. Rising behind the four, large and dramatic but not too scary, are Filipino folklore creatures: a giant bakunawa sea serpent coiling across the sky around a pale moon, a tikbalang with a horse head and long legs standing in the mist on the right, a tall kapre sitting in a big tree on the left, a diwata nature spirit with soft glowing butterfly wings floating near the top, a colorful sarimanok bird with long tail feathers flying above, and a faint flying manananggal silhouette in the clouds. Golden ribbons of Baybayin style script curl through the air around the characters, with no readable words.

Text on the cover: the title HIRAYA in large bold letters across the top, drawn in a style inspired by Baybayin script but spelled in English letters, reading exactly H I R A Y A. In the lower right corner, a small neat label reading exactly Chapter 1. No other text.

Avoid school uniforms, realistic or photographic rendering, 3D rendering, extra fingers, deformed hands, blurry results, ninja outfits, headbands that look like ninja headbands, scary gore, misspelled title text, and copies of any existing characters.
```

## Notes
- If the creatures crowd the characters, ask for fewer creatures: keep the bakunawa, the tikbalang and the diwata.
- If the title comes out misspelled, use an edit: "Keep everything the same. Fix the title so it reads exactly H I R A Y A."
- If a character's face drifts, attach only that character's sheet and the first result, and ask for that character to be fixed.
