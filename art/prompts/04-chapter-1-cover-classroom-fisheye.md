## Prompt
```
A vertical manga chapter cover illustration, aspect ratio 2:3, in full color, hand drawn with bold ink linework, flat cel style coloring with simple shading, in the style of a classic shonen manga cover like Naruto. It must clearly look like a drawing, not realistic, not 3D, not photographic.

Reference images: the first attached image is the HIRAYA logo. The next six are the character sheets of Jeff, Hal, Mel, Nikki, Sir Archie and Totet. Draw each character exactly as on their sheet: same face, hair, skin tone, clothes and body type. Use the sheets for the look only, do not copy the sheet poses or the sheet expressions. Each face must be animated and show a clear emotion that fits this moment, with the head turned and the eyes looking where described below.

Perspective: an extreme fisheye lens view, with strong barrel distortion, the whole classroom of Durian City High School curving around the edges of the picture as if seen through a fisheye lens from a low, close camera. The four main characters are in the foreground, very close to the camera, leaning in, so their hands and faces are large and slightly stretched by the lens. The classroom bends behind them. Everyone wears the school uniform (boys in blue short sleeved buttoned shirts and khaki pants, girls in white blouse and skirt), except Sir Archie.

Foreground, the four main characters, with these expressions:
Jeff in the front center: wide eyed and curious, eyebrows raised, completely focused on the glowing bamboo tube on his desk, head turned down toward it. Both arms reach forward over the desk, each with one elbow, one hand resting on a thick folklore book and the other hand flat on the desk.
Hal on Jeff's left: startled and excited, eyes wide, mouth open in a whisper shout. His right arm is stretched toward the camera in one long straight line with the hand open and waving, one elbow only, and his other hand holds the desk.
Mel on Jeff's right: soft and dreamy, eyes gently closed, a warm smile, holding his wooden violin against his chest with both arms, one hand under the violin and one hand on its neck, each arm with one elbow.
Nikki beside them: cocky and amused, one eyebrow up, laughing behind her hand while looking at the viewer. Her arm pose in plain steps: her right arm hangs from the shoulder, which sits right next to her neck, down to ONE elbow that rests on the desk, and ONE forearm goes up from that elbow to her hand, which covers her mouth. Her left arm rests on her lap or on the desk edge. Exactly two arms, each with exactly one elbow.
Sizes: Mel is the tallest and biggest, Jeff is the second tallest, Nikki is shorter, and Hal is the shortest.

Midground and background: a bright Philippine classroom during a lesson, with ceiling fans, open windows with palm trees outside, wooden desks in rows, and about fifteen other students at the desks, each with different faces, hairstyles and clear different emotions: some bored, some sleepy, some whispering, some laughing, some staring at Jeff. Totet sits at the desk behind Jeff, blushing, glancing at him, then looking away shyly. At the front stands Sir Archie, writing on the chalkboard and half turned to the class, with a calm, cool, teacherly smile.

The chalkboard behind Sir Archie curves in the fisheye view and is covered in chalk lessons about ancient Philippine history: a hand drawn map of the islands, a balangay boat with a striped sail, drawings of gold ornaments and a gold funerary mask, small Baybayin style script characters, a timeline arrow, and a sketch of a river port with stilt houses. The chalkboard heading, written in chalk at the top, reads exactly ANCIENT BUTUAN. No other readable words on the chalkboard.

A faint hint of magic: the bamboo tube on Jeff's desk has a soft golden glow, and a few thin golden ribbons of Baybayin style script float up from it, with no readable words.

Text on the cover: at the top, use the attached HIRAYA logo exactly as it is, the same brush ink letters with the small mark beside the H, only scaled to fit, do not redraw or restyle it and keep it spelled H I R A Y A. Directly under the logo, a smaller subtitle line reading exactly Chapter 1: Banaag. At the bottom left corner, small neat credit text reading exactly Authored by Claude and Miko Daray. No other text except the chalkboard heading.

Anatomy must be correct: every person has exactly two arms, each arm with exactly one elbow and exactly two segments (upper arm and forearm, never three segments), exactly two hands, each hand has exactly five fingers (four fingers and one thumb), and exactly two legs, with no extra, missing, merged, or duplicated arms, hands, fingers, or legs. Count the limbs and fingers of every person, including people in the background. Avoid arms with three segments, two elbows or extra joints, the same expression on every character, realistic or photographic rendering, 3D rendering, extra or missing fingers, more than five fingers on a hand, more than two arms, a third arm, extra or duplicate arms, hands, or legs, merged limbs, deformed hands, blurry results, ninja outfits, headbands, a redrawn or changed logo, misspelled text, any other readable words, and copies of any existing characters.
```
