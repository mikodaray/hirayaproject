# Chapter 1 cover prompt, version 3: classroom fisheye

Status: DRAFT. Made 2026-11-06 at the creator's request, a second cover for Chapter 1.
Aspect ratio: 2:3 portrait. Covers stay in full color (see art/ART-STYLE.md).
Attach as references: character sheets for Jeff, Hal, Mel, Nikki, Sir Archie and Totet from art/characters/. The classmates in the background are generic students, only Totet is a named classmate.
Chalkboard lessons are about ancient Philippine history, flavor from story-bible/05-research-notes.md. Only one short chalk heading has words so the text is less likely to come out wrong.

## Prompt
```
A vertical manga chapter cover illustration, aspect ratio 2:3, in full color, hand drawn with bold ink linework, expressive faces, and flat cel style coloring with simple shading, in the style of a classic shonen manga cover like Naruto. It must clearly look like a drawing, not realistic, not 3D, not photographic. Use the attached character sheets for the faces, hair, clothes and body types of the named characters.

Perspective: an extreme fisheye lens view, with strong barrel distortion, the whole classroom curving around the edges of the picture as if seen through a fisheye lens from a low, close camera. The four main characters are in the foreground, very close to the camera, leaning in, so their hands and faces are large and slightly stretched by the lens. The classroom bends behind them.

Foreground, the four main characters in their school uniforms (boys in blue short sleeved buttoned shirts and khaki pants, girls in white blouse and skirt): Jeff in the front center, tall and lean, messy ube purple hair, calm confident smirk, looking at the viewer sideways, a small stone pendant necklace glowing softly under his collar, one hand resting on a thick folklore book. Hal on his left, short, neat combed brown hair, round glasses, waving a hand toward the camera with a startled and excited face. Mel on his right, big and gentle with long golden blonde hair, holding his wooden violin against his chest with a soft smile. Nikki beside them, long wavy brown hair, turned toward the camera in her seat with a cocky smirk and a hand on her hip. Sizes: Mel is the tallest and biggest, Jeff is the second tallest, Nikki is shorter, and Hal is the shortest.

Midground and background: a bright Philippine classroom during a lesson, with ceiling fans, open windows with palm trees outside, wooden desks in rows, and about fifteen other students at the desks, each with different faces, hairstyles and reactions, some taking notes, some whispering, some looking sleepy, some looking at Jeff. Totet, a shy girl with a black bob cut and freckles on her cheeks, sits at the desk behind Jeff, secretly glancing at him with a small blush. At the front of the classroom stands Sir Archie, the teacher, a tall muscular young man with messy white hair, rectangle glasses, a fitted buttoned cream shirt and a brown cardigan, holding a piece of chalk and pointing at the chalkboard with a calm, cool smile.

The chalkboard behind Sir Archie curves in the fisheye view and is covered in chalk lessons about ancient Philippine history: a hand drawn map of the islands, a balangay boat with a striped sail, drawings of gold ornaments and a gold funerary mask, small Baybayin style script characters, a timeline arrow, and a sketch of a river port with stilt houses. The chalkboard heading, written in chalk at the top, reads exactly ANCIENT BUTUAN. No other readable words on the chalkboard.

A faint hint of magic: a bamboo tube sits on Jeff's desk with a soft golden glow, and a few thin golden ribbons of Baybayin style script float up from it, with no readable words.

Text on the cover, spelled exactly: the title HIRAYA in large bold letters across the top, drawn in a style inspired by Baybayin script but spelled in English letters, reading exactly H I R A Y A. Directly under the title, a smaller subtitle line reading exactly Chapter 1: Banaag. At the bottom left corner, small neat credit text reading exactly Authored by Claude and Miko Daray. No other text except the chalkboard heading.

Anatomy must be correct: every person has exactly two arms and exactly two hands, each hand has exactly five fingers (four fingers and one thumb), and exactly two legs, with no extra, missing, merged, or duplicated arms, hands, fingers, or legs. Count the limbs and fingers of every person, including people in the background. Avoid realistic or photographic rendering, 3D rendering, extra or missing fingers, more than five fingers on a hand, more than two arms, a third arm, extra or duplicate arms, hands, or legs, merged limbs, deformed hands, blurry results, ninja outfits, headbands, misspelled text, any other readable words, and copies of any existing characters.
```

## Notes
- If the fisheye is too weak, add: "Exaggerate the fisheye barrel distortion even more, the edges of the classroom should bend strongly."
- If the crowd looks copied, ask for "every student has a clearly different face and hairstyle".
- If the chalkboard heading comes out misspelled, use an edit: "Keep everything the same. Fix the chalkboard heading to read exactly ANCIENT BUTUAN."
