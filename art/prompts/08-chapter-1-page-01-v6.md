Bold shonen manga style close to the classic Naruto manga, expressive faces, dynamic perspective. Clean white gutters between panels. Panels read LEFT TO RIGHT, top to bottom.

Add NO other text anywhere, no name labels, no speech bubbles, no page numbers, no readable signage except the school sign, no watermark, no signature.

Avoid color, glasses on Jeff, a second Jeff, duplicated or cloned background figures, realistic, 3D or photographic rendering, panel borders drawn inside a panel, misspelled sound effects, and copies of any existing characters.

Final anatomy pass before rendering: inspect every visible character and remove any extra limbs, extra fingers, duplicate hands, merged hands, phantom hands, or malformed joints.
